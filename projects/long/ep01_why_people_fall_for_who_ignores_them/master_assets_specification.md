## 1. Core Visual Grammar & Color Discipline

To sustain high audience retention over a 10–14 minute runtime without visual exhaustion, the visual design must follow a strict, disciplined ruleset:

### The Color Palette Rule (Strict Tri-Color Accenting):
* **Canvas / Ground (`#FAF9F6`):** Warm, tactile ivory paper. Never pure digital white `#FFFFFF` (which causes eye strain) and never dark mode (which destroys the hand-drawn ink notebook feel). Subtle paper grain texture.
* **Line Art (`#111111`):** Deep solid India ink vector strokes with a constant stroke weight of **6px to 8px**. Hand-drawn micro-imperfections, zero computerized plastic smoothing.
* **🔴 Accent 1: Crimson Red (`#FF3B30`):**
  * *Meaning:* Anxiety, cognitive distortion, desperation, frantic text notifications, slot machine warning, pedestal danger.
  * *Usage Rule:* Used sparingly (max 10% of any frame) to highlight the psychological trap or the frantic character's chaotic energy.
* **🟡 Accent 2: Warm Amber / Gold (`#F59E0B`):**
  * *Meaning:* High perceived value, scarcity, dopamine spark, electric curiosity, aura of self-respect.
  * *Usage Rule:* Highlights precious commodities (gold nuggets, the rare reply notification, the inner glow of the detached mind).
* **🔵 Accent 3: Deep Slate Blue (`#2563EB`):**
  * *Meaning:* Scientific frameworks, B.F. Skinner's laboratory mechanics, economics curves, objective social laws.
  * *Usage Rule:* Used in diagrams, balance scales, and chapter title cards.

## 2. Master Character DNA Sheets (Consistency Locks)

To guarantee that characters remain 100% consistent across all 148+ storyboard shots, each character has a fixed design silhouette, color key, and physical posture logic.

### Character 1: The Sovereign / The Detached Individual (`CHAR_01_SOVEREIGN`)
* **Role:** The psychological anchor and protagonist. Represents emotional self-sufficiency, composure, and quiet sovereignty.
* **Head & Face:** Smooth round white circle head, crisp black ink outline. Hair is a clean, slightly tousled dark charcoal fringe over his forehead. Calm, horizontal dot eyes; relaxed, unraised horizontal ink brows. Never panics, never squints, never shows nervous sweat droplets.
* **Body & Clothing:** Slightly oversized dark charcoal-black cotton hoodie (`#1F2421`) with clean ink fold creases. Hands are almost always tucked casually in his front kangaroo pocket or holding a notebook/coffee cup. Simple black stick legs with clean white doodle sneakers.
* **Posture & Gravity:** Upright, grounded center of gravity. When sitting, he leans back into the chair; when standing, his weight is balanced evenly. He never leans forward to enter someone else's personal space.
* **Visual Metaphor:** An unshakeable mountain or a quiet planet with its own steady gravitational pull.

---

### Character 2: The Eager Texter / The Over-Giver (`CHAR_02_OVERGIVER`)
* **Role:** The visual embodiment of the Pedestal Paradox and unconditional availability. Represents social desperation, people-pleasing, and validation anxiety.
* **Head & Face:** Round white head with neatly parted comb-over hair. Wide, dilated cartoon dot eyes; curved, high, anxious eyebrows. Tiny comic sweat droplets (`#38BDF8` or red `#FF3B30`) frequently floating near his temple. Often seen with a wide, forced, eager smile that looks exhausting to maintain.
* **Body & Clothing:** Light teal/cyan long-sleeve sweater (`#0284C7`), dark stick legs, tidy dark shoes. Neck is often tilted forward in a slight hunch.
* **Posture & Gesture:** Frantically clutching a glowing red-tinted smartphone with both hands at chest height. Leaning forward aggressively into other characters' space, nodding rapidly, or waving arms to gain attention.
* **Visual Metaphor:** A frantic moth circling an electric bulb, rapidly burning out its own wings.

---

### Character 3: The Female Observer / Romantic Counterpart (`CHAR_03_OBSERVER`)
* **Role:** The audience mirror and relational counterpart. Experiences the emotional shift from feeling overwhelmed by needy behavior to being magnetically intrigued by quiet detachment.
* **Head & Face:** Smooth round white head, crisp ink outline. Dark hair tied into a neat high ponytail with a black band, with a small soft fringe framing her face. Highly expressive eyes:
  * *State A (With Over-Giver):* Heavy-lidded, downward glance, slight grimace or overwhelmed sigh (represented by a deflating comic puff).
  * *State B (With Sovereign):* Wide, curious dot eyes, slight head tilt, looking over her shoulder with an arched eyebrow of curiosity.
* **Body & Clothing:** Warm pastel beige knit sweater (`#E5D9C5`), simple black stick legs, clean white sneakers. Often carries a minimalist single-strap canvas tote bag or notebook.
* **Posture & Gesture:** Relaxed, natural posture. Transitions from pulling back her shoulders when crowded by the over-giver, to leaning forward slightly when observing the sovereign boy across the library.

---

### Character 4: The Pushy Salesman (`CHAR_04_SALESMAN`)

* **Design:** Exaggerated wide grin with visible teeth lines, loud striped necktie, holding oversized cardboard discount signs (`"-50% OFF!"`, `"PLEASE BUY!"`), megaphone in hand, chasing reluctant shoppers.

### Character 5: Professor / Lab Scientist (`CHAR_05_SCIENTIST`)
* **Role:** Represents B.F. Skinner in Chapter 2 (The Casino Effect).
* **Design:** Clean white lab coat over a dark shirt, round professor spectacles, clipboard with graph notes, calm academic demeanor.

## 3. Master Environment Anchors (16:9 Widescreen Plates)

All environments are drawn in **16:9 Widescreen (`1920x1080`)** with clean 2D doodle architecture, zero 3D photorealism, and zero distracting clutter.

### Anchor 1: The Late-Night Bedroom / Phone Desk (`ENV_01_BEDROOM`)
* **Canvas Elements:** Clean wooden study desk on the lower third. A minimalist desk lamp casting a warm amber puddle of light (`#FEF3C7`). A glowing smartphone face illuminated on the desk showing `02:14 AM`. In the background, a simple window frame showing a dark slate night sky with a minimalist crescent moon.
* **Emotional Mood:** Quiet isolation, insomnia, the obsessive 2:00 AM screen stare.

### Anchor 2: The B.F. Skinner Laboratory Box (`ENV_02_SKINNER_LAB`)
* **Canvas Elements:** A clinical, minimalist cutaway view of an experimental animal chamber. On the left wall, a polished mechanical lever with a red indicator light. In the center, a simple food dispenser cup. Clean slate-blue grid lines on the wall to evoke laboratory precision.
* **Emotional Mood:** Scientific, detached, revelatory.

### Anchor 3: The University Library Sanctuary (`ENV_03_LIBRARY`)
* **Canvas Elements:** Tall floor-to-ceiling wooden bookshelves in soft charcoal outlines filled with books. A long solid oak study table extending horizontally across the frame. Classic green-shaded banker’s lamps. Soft warm paper daylight streaming through tall arched windows.
* **Emotional Mood:** Deep focus, intellectual calm, sacred silence.

### Anchor 4: The Bustling Campus Corridor & Café (`ENV_04_CAMPUS_CAFE`)
* **Canvas Elements:** Clean wooden café tables, minimalist coffee cups with rising doodle steam lines. In the background, faint blurred silhouettes of walking students creating social density without distracting from the foreground characters.
* **Emotional Mood:** Social pressure, casual observation, public eye.

### Anchor 5: The Abstract Mindspace / The Psychological Blackboard (`ENV_05_ABSTRACT_MIND`)
* **Canvas Elements:** Pristine paper canvas featuring floating conceptual graphics:
  * The Brass Balance Scale (measuring Investment vs Leverage).
  * The Supply & Demand Curves (intersecting with red and gold arrows).
  * The Dopamine Synapse Circuit (anticipation spark vs reward plateau).
  * The Pedestal & The Fall (a 10-foot marble column with a tiny character teetering at the edge).
* **Emotional Mood:** Epiphany, philosophical clarity, intellectual mastery.

## 4. Cinematic Camera & Pacing Grammar for 10+ Minutes

1. **The Dynamic Hold (No Static Slides):**
   * Static slides cause instant drop-off. In our rendering pipeline, every single 16:9 visual beat will have a continuous, silky-smooth **camera drift (slow 1.00x to 1.04x zoom-in)** or **horizontal pan**, making the hand-drawn ink feel alive.
2. **The Contrast Split-Screen:**
   * Used frequently to highlight the core psychological thesis:
     * *Left Frame (Crimson Red Aura):* The desperate texter sweating over his phone at 2:00 AM.
     * *Right Frame (Golden Calm Aura):* The sovereign sleeping soundly or focused on his craft.
3. **The Typographic Punch Card:**
   * Every 2 to 3 minutes, a critical thesis statement is hammered home with a bold, hand-drawn typographic visual card (e.g. `"THE PEDESTAL EFFECT"`, `"DOPAMINE IS ANTICIPATION"`, `"SUPPLY CREATES SCARCITY"`).
   * Holds for exactly 1.5 to 2.2 seconds accompanied by a heavy acoustic drum thud.
