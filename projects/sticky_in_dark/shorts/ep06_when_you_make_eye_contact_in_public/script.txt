The second eye contact gets awkward with someone walking toward you, what’s the first thing you do?
You whip out your phone, staring intensely at a blank screen like you got an urgent text.
Be honest, we’ve all done it.
You spot someone twenty meters away, and your brain panics.
It’s too far to say hello, but too close to pretend they don't exist.
If you stare at them the entire way, you look unhinged.
If you stare down at your shoes, you look terrified.
So you unlock your phone, pretending to check notifications that don't exist.
Look, the other person watches you do it. They know you're faking—because they were tempted to do the exact same thing.
Here’s what to do instead.
Leave the phone in your pocket.
Keep your shoulders relaxed, eyes focused on where you’re walking.
At three steps away, look up, give an easy nod, and keep moving.
It acknowledges them without forcing awkward small talk.
No awkward staring, no fake texting, and no panic.
