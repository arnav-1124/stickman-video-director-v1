# Sticky in Dark: Director Storyboard
## Episode 07: Dopamine Is Not What You Think
**Format:** YouTube Shorts (9:16 Vertical / 1080x1920)  
**Total Production Beats:** 20 Master Comic Beats  
**Total Runtime:** **52.50 Seconds** (Locked to `audio/master_voiceover.wav`)  
**Pacing:** ~2.62s Average Slide Density (Max Static Hold $\le 3.5s$)  
**Art Style:** Signature Sticky in Dark 2D Comic Noir (Warm Textured Cream Paper Canvas `#FAF9F6`)  
**Character Anchor:** Protagonist (White circular head, messy textured dark-brown fringe, dark-forest green hoodie `#1B4332`)

## 🎬 Act Breakdown & Beat Architecture

### 🔴 ACT 1: THE MIDNIGHT TRAP (The Hook) | 0.00s – 6.31s

* **Beat 01 (0.00s – 1.15s | Duration: 1.15s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Before you scroll,"*
  * **Scene Type:** Kinetic Comic Hook (Wide/Medium)
  * **Visual Description:** Protagonist in his dark green hoodie lies propped up on his bed in the evening. His smartphone beams a sharp, cool electric-blue glow onto his face. A bold hand-drawn hand reaches up with a stop gesture in the foreground.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_cozy_bedroom` + `stickman_scrolling_phone` + `blue_screen_glow`

* **Beat 02 (1.15s – 4.15s | Duration: 3.00s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"dopamine is not the pleasure chemical you think it is."*
  * **Scene Type:** Text & Diagram Punch Card
  * **Visual Description:** Clean educational blackboard graphic on warm cream paper. Crisp white vector brain cutaway on the left. On the right, a cartoon chemical flask filled with bubbling gold liquid labeled `[ PLEASURE? ]`. A bold Crimson Red slash stamps across it with hand-drawn text: `NO.`
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_cream_grid` + `brain_diagram_cutaway` + `flask_pleasure_slashed`

* **Beat 03 (4.15s – 6.31s | Duration: 2.16s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"You think it makes you feel happy, but it doesn't."*
  * **Scene Type:** Dramatic Close-Up Reaction
  * **Visual Description:** Extreme close-up on the protagonist's face. Eyes wide and tired, mouth completely flat and unsmiling. Floating beside his cheek is a yellow cartoon smiley face bubble, violently struck out with a thick hand-drawn Crimson Red "✕".
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_subtle_screentone` + `protagonist_deadpan_face` + `smiley_bubble_struck_out`

### 🟡 ACT 2: THE TWO-HOUR SPIRAL | 6.31s – 13.91s

* **Beat 04 (6.31s – 10.10s | Duration: 3.79s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"If dopamine was pleasure, you would feel satisfied after scrolling for two hours."*
  * **Scene Type:** Time-Lapse Narrative Comic
  * **Visual Description:** Side view of the protagonist slumped on the couch. Fast-forward motion arcs: on the wall behind him, a minimalist round wall clock spins wildly from `11:00 PM` $\rightarrow$ `1:00 AM`. A cascade of ghosted cartoon phone video cards floats in the air around him.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_living_room_couch` + `stickman_slumped_scrolling` + `spinning_wall_clock`

* **Beat 05 (10.10s – 13.91s | Duration: 3.81s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Instead, you feel completely empty, foggy, and exhausted."*
  * **Scene Type:** Emotional Deflation Punch
  * **Visual Description:** The protagonist sits hunched over the edge of the mattress, shoulders collapsed in weary posture, eyes half-lidded with dark exhaustion circles. Above his head floats a cartoon battery icon with a blinking red rim reading `[ 1% ]`. A faint white cartoon puff of breath drifts from his lips.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_bedroom_night` + `protagonist_slumped_deflated` + `empty_battery_indicator`

### 🔬 ACT 3: THE NEUROSCIENCE REVEAL | 13.91s – 21.06s

* **Beat 06 (13.91s – 16.20s | Duration: 2.29s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Neuroscientists proved this decades ago:"*
  * **Scene Type:** Scientific Blueprint Panel
  * **Visual Description:** Vintage green chalkboard on cream paper. A minimalist cartoon stickman scientist wearing small round glasses and a lab coat stands beside a retro anatomical brain diagram, pointing firmly with a wooden pointer stick.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_vintage_chalkboard` + `stickman_scientist_pointer` + `retro_brain_diagram`

* **Beat 07 (16.20s – 18.50s | Duration: 2.30s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"dopamine doesn't spike when you get a reward."*
  * **Scene Type:** Concept Metaphor Panel
  * **Visual Description:** A beautifully wrapped cardboard gift box with a yellow ribbon labeled `[ REWARD ]` sits open on a table. Below it on a timeline axis, the dopamine line is completely flat and lifeless: `--- FLATLINE ---`.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_minimal_lab_table` + `open_reward_box` + `flatline_graph_axis`

* **Beat 08 (18.50s – 21.06s | Duration: 2.56s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"It spikes right before—during the anticipation."*
  * **Scene Type:** Kinetic Graph Climax
  * **Visual Description:** The stickman's cartoon mitten hand hovers 2 inches above the closed box, trembling with anticipation. At this exact millimeter before touching the box, a violent, jagged Crimson Red graph spike surges straight up to the top of the panel labeled `[ ANTICIPATION! ]` with electric sparks.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_cream_paper` + `hovering_hand_over_box` + `surging_red_graph_spike`

### 🏃‍♂️ ACT 4: THE ENDLESS TREADMILL | 21.06s – 28.21s

* **Beat 09 (21.06s – 23.40s | Duration: 2.34s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"It is not satisfaction. It is an itch."*
  * **Scene Type:** Psychological Metaphor Punch
  * **Visual Description:** Graphic cutaway of the protagonist's circular white head. Inside the brain, dozens of red comic prickle lines and exclamation points vibrate like an unbearable rash. Large bold hand-drawn text above: `THE ITCH`.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_cream_canvas` + `protagonist_head_cutaway` + `red_prickle_rash_lines`

* **Beat 10 (23.40s – 25.10s | Duration: 1.70s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"A biological itch that whispers:"*
  * **Scene Type:** Dramatic Character Contrast
  * **Visual Description:** Close side-profile of the protagonist. Looming directly behind his shoulder in the dark is a shadowy stick figure with mischievous grin and red outlined eyes, whispering directly into his ear with a curly speech bubble: `*pssst...*`.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_dark_shadow_gradient` + `protagonist_hesitant_profile` + `whispering_shadow_demon`

* **Beat 11 (25.10s – 28.21s | Duration: 3.11s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"just one more video, just one more click, maybe the next one is better."*
  * **Scene Type:** The Endless Treadmill Metaphor
  * **Visual Description:** Side view of the protagonist running frantically on a revolving circular hamster-wheel treadmill. Dangling from a fishing rod strapped to his back is a glowing golden YouTube Short play button (#FFD700), hovering inches beyond his grasping fingertips.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_minimalist_floor` + `stickman_running_treadmill` + `dangling_golden_play_button`

### 🎰 ACT 5: THE POCKET CASINO | 28.21s – 37.00s

* **Beat 12 (28.21s – 32.10s | Duration: 3.89s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Every time you refresh, your brain floods with craving, not fulfillment."*
  * **Scene Type:** Micro-Action Verb Panel
  * **Visual Description:** Tight close-up of a cartoon thumb pulling down on a smartphone screen. A circular loading spinner turns furiously. Sharp crimson arrows flood into a cartoon brain icon above the phone labeled `[ CRAVING ]`, while a small grey gauge labeled `[ SATISFACTION ]` reads zero.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_hand_holding_phone` + `pull_to_refresh_spinner` + `dual_gauges_craving_vs_satisfaction`

* **Beat 13 (32.10s – 34.40s | Duration: 2.30s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Cheap apps hijacked this ancient hunting reflex,"*
  * **Scene Type:** Evolutionary Split-Screen
  * **Visual Description:** Vertical split panel:
    * Left: Ancient caveman stickman in animal pelt tracking animal tracks in the wild with spear in hand.
    * Right: Modern stickman in green hoodie hunched over glowing smartphone tracking red notification badges.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `split_screen_ancient_vs_modern` + `caveman_hunter` + `modern_scroller`

* **Beat 14 (34.40s – 37.00s | Duration: 2.60s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"turning your focus into a slot machine in your pocket."*
  * **Scene Type:** The Slot Machine Metaphor
  * **Visual Description:** A giant smartphone standing upright like a Vegas slot machine. A mechanical red lever on the side snaps down. The screen reels spin with motion blur, settling on `[ 🔔 ] [ ❤️ ] [ 💬 ]`. The protagonist stands beside it, mesmerized by the neon lights.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_casino_neon_glow` + `giant_phone_slot_machine` + `protagonist_hypnotized`

### 🛡️ ACT 6: THE 20-SECOND RESET | 37.00s – 46.56s

* **Beat 15 (37.00s – 38.60s | Duration: 1.60s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Here’s how you take control."*
  * **Scene Type:** Sovereign Stance Shift
  * **Visual Description:** Medium shot of the protagonist. He turns away from the phone, standing upright and grounded. Chest lifted, arms crossed calmly, eyes focused and determined. Clean minimalist room with morning sunlight beginning to cut through.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_morning_room` + `protagonist_grounded_stance` + `sunlight_beam_diagonal`

* **Beat 16 (38.60s – 41.50s | Duration: 2.90s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Put twenty seconds of friction between you and cheap dopamine."*
  * **Scene Type:** Tactical Rule Card
  * **Visual Description:** High-impact graphic card on cream paper: A bold Emerald Green stopwatch shield labeled `[ 20 SECONDS ]` (#2A9D8F) stands like a physical barrier between the stickman and his smartphone. Hand-drawn text: `THE FRICTION PROTOCOL`.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_cream_grid` + `green_stopwatch_shield` + `phone_locked_behind_barrier`

* **Beat 17 (41.50s – 43.10s | Duration: 1.60s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"Put the phone in another room,"*
  * **Scene Type:** Physical Action Beat
  * **Visual Description:** Protagonist places his black phone face-down on a wooden desk in an empty bedroom, then walks through the doorway into the hallway, leaving the device behind.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_bedroom_doorway` + `phone_face_down_on_desk` + `stickman_walking_away`

* **Beat 18 (43.10s – 46.56s | Duration: 3.46s)**
  * **Spoken Clause:** *"take ten deep breaths, and let the craving burn out."*
  * **Scene Type:** Somatic Release / Breath Reset
  * **Visual Description:** Wide, tranquil shot. Protagonist stands by an open window with soft morning sunlight pouring over him. Chest expanded in a deep, peaceful inhale. Above his head, a tiny flickering red cartoon flame gently fizzles out into a wisp of white smoke.
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_open_window_sunlight` + `protagonist_deep_breath` + `fizzling_craving_flame`

A subtle warm golden halo radiates behind his head. The frame holds in sovereign quiet... cutting seamlessly right back into *"Before you scroll..."*!
  * **Composition / Layer:** `bg_tranquil_cream_void` + `protagonist_meditating_bench` + `golden_sovereignty_halo`
