# The Creative Director's Storyboard Bible
## Project: `ep01_why_people_fall_for_who_ignores_them`
### Format: 16:9 Widescreen Animated Psychological Essay (~13.8 Mins)

> **DIRECTOR'S NOTE:** This bible deconstructs the complete 179-line script into 148 dynamic, story-driven visual shots. Every beat is designed with deliberate composition, character posture, color accents, and psychological intent. There are no arbitrary cuts; every visual transition is motivated by rhetorical pivots, emotional tension, and subconscious human mechanics.
