# Channel Production Guidelines: Sticky in Dark

> **Applicable for:** `projects/sticky_in_dark/` (Shorts & Long-Form)  
> **Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

---

## 1. Title & Packaging Principles
* **Simple, Short & Everyday Language:**
  * Titles must be clear, natural, and under **42 characters** (mobile-safe without `...` truncation).
  * **Strictly Banned:** Technical jargon, academic labels, and formulaic "The X Rule" / "The Y Method" naming (e.g., NO "The 3-Second Rule", NO "The Gray Rock Method", NO "The Hallway Rule").
  * **Strictly Banned Tropes:** `Alpha`, `Sigma`, `Dark Psychology`, `High Value Male`, `Hierarchy`.
  * **Format:** Everyday visceral questions or relatable situations (e.g., `When You Make Eye Contact In Public #shorts`, `Why You Rehearse Arguments In Bed #shorts`).

---

## 2. Story Arc & Hook Architecture
* **1.5-Second Title Congruence (Anti-Swipe Protocol):**
  * Cut 1 (0.0s – 1.5s) must immediately depict the core subject and tension promised in the title. Never delay introducing the scenario.
* **The Voice Persona:**
  * The grounded, experienced senior classmate or wise older brother.
  * Warm, observant, witty, and candid. Zero preachy, robotic, or clinical lecturing.
* **5-Phase Narrative Flow:**
  1. **Immediate Dilemma (0–3s):** Frame 1 high-contrast visual + visceral question.
  2. **The Relatable Trap (3–12s):** The awkward mistake most people make (e.g. fake texting, nervous smiling).
  3. **The Counter-Intuitive Pivot (12–22s):** Senior reframing the dynamic.
  4. **The Actionable Truth (22–38s):** Exactly how to handle it with calm body language.
  5. **Seamless Audio Loop (38–48s):** Final sentence connects syntactically straight back into the opening line for >100% APV.

---

## 3. Output Format & Technical Standards
* **Duration:** Strictly **under 50 seconds** (target: 44s–48s, word count: 120–135 words).
* **Format:** Vertical 9:16 (`1080x1920` or `1536x2752`), 60 FPS.
* **Aesthetic:** Minimalist noir stickman on high-contrast backgrounds with selective canary yellow/crimson accents.
* **Banned Setting Terms:** The generic words `hallway`, `walkway`, and `pathway` are strictly prohibited (use natural settings: *across campus, street, sidewalk, college courtyard, library, café*).
