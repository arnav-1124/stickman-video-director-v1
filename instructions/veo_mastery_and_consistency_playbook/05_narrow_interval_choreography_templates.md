# Chapter 05: Narrow-Interval Choreography & Master Templates

> "Diffusion models thrive on micro-instructions. If you don't direct second 3, the AI will invent second 3."

The single most effective technique for eliminating character distortion and temporal drift in Veo 3.1 is **Narrow-Interval Micro-Timing (1s–2s intervals)**.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of an 8-Second Directorial Block

Instead of writing a broad paragraph, break the 8 seconds down into **5 distinct chronological phases**:

| Time Window | Directorial Function | What to Specify |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0s – 1s** | **Initiation & Camera Vector** | Starting camera move, initial character eye direction, weight shift. |
| **1s – 3s** | **Primary Locomotion / Action** | Core physical movement, foot strides, limb gestures, prop interaction. |
| **3s – 5s** | **Climax Beat & Spoken Dialogue** | Main dramatic beat, key dialogue line, peak facial expression. |
| **5s – 7s** | **Secondary Reaction & Settlement** | Companion character reaction, physical follow-through, environmental response. |
| **7s – 8s** | **Hold & Edit Transition** | Action settles into final pose, camera decelerates, preparing clean cut. |

---

## 2. Master Production Templates (Ready to Copy-Paste)

### Template 1: Investigation / Detective Discovery Beat
```text
For 8 seconds:
[Visual Motion]:
- 0s-1s: The camera starts at ground level, slowly dollying forward along the path. [Character A] crouches low to the ground with focused eyes.
- 1s-3s: [Character A] runs their hand over the turf, their brow furrowing in deep concentration. Beside them, [Character B] hovers, casting a soft cyan scanner glow over the ground.
- 3s-5s: [Character A] spots the clue on the ground, their eyes widening with sharp realization as they point directly forward with an excited shout.
- 5s-7s: [Character C] takes two quick running steps forward, crouching right beside [Character A] to inspect the clue.
- 7s-8s: Both characters look up toward the horizon with determined expressions as the camera gently holds. Only the characters in the starting frame animate.
[Audio Cues]:
- Dialogue (at 3s): [Character A]: "Look! Right over here!"
- Sound Effects: Soft grass rustle, electronic scanner hum, quick footsteps on turf.
- Ambience: Quiet, mysterious outdoor breeze.
```

---

### Template 2: Emotional Intimate Close-Up Beat
```text
For 8 seconds:
[Visual Motion]:
- 0s-2s: The camera executes an intimate, slow push-in toward [Character's] face. [Character] clutches [Prop] gently against their chest, blinking slowly.
- 2s-4s: [Character] looks across the quiet empty space. Their eyelids droop with profound loneliness and their ears/shoulders sag downward.
- 4s-6s: [Character's] chest rises and falls with a deep, heavy, heartbreaking sigh, a single tear glistening in their warm brown eyes.
- 6s-8s: The camera rack-focuses slightly to [Secondary Character] standing in the background, who softly places a hand over their heart with pure empathy and compassion. No new characters appear.
[Audio Cues]:
- Dialogue (at 6s): [Secondary Character]: "He just wants friends to play with."
- Sound Effects: Gentle rustle of fabric, deep soft creature sigh ("Huuuuh...").
- Ambience: Tender, emotional, quiet outdoor stillness.
```

---

### Template 3: Dynamic Physical Action / Sports Pass
```text
For 8 seconds:
[Visual Motion]:
- 0s-2s: Low-angle dynamic camera tracking forward near turf level. [Character A] takes two energetic running strides across the grass with a beaming, confident grin.
- 2s-4s: [Character A] plants their left foot and swings their right leg in a clean, solid kick, sending [the ball] rolling smoothly across the grass toward [Character B].
- 4s-6s: As the ball arrives, [Character B] blinks in surprise, then gently traps the ball beneath their massive soft foot.
- 6s-8s: [Character B]'s face bursts into an enormous, radiant, ecstatic ear-to-ear smile! [Character A] pumps their fist in celebration as the camera gently tilts up.
[Audio Cues]:
- Dialogue (at 2s): [Character A]: "Pass it here, buddy!"
- Sound Effects: Fast footsteps on grass, crisp solid ball kick, happy rumbling creature giggle.
- Ambience: Sunny, energetic, joyful outdoor ambience.
```

---

### Template 4: Grand Triumphant Celebration
```text
For 8 seconds:
[Visual Motion]:
- 0s-2s: The camera begins at medium height and smoothly crane-elevates upward while pulling back, revealing the full bustling sunlit field.
- 2s-4s: [Central Character] stands proudly in the center with [Character A and B] perched happily on their shoulders waving triumphantly to the cheering crowd.
- 4s-6s: Surrounding children jump and bounce on their feet with pure joy, hugging [Central Character's] legs and kicking soccer balls toward the goal net.
- 6s-8s: Colorful celebratory confetti and warm golden sunlight glint through the air as [Central Character] sways happily with an ecstatic, toothy grin. No new characters appear.
[Audio Cues]:
- Dialogue (at 2s): All characters cheering in unison: "Hooray! Best friend ever!"
- Sound Effects: Massive joyful crowd cheering, rhythmic clapping, soccer kicks, referee whistle.
- Ambience: Vibrant, triumphant community festival atmosphere.
```
