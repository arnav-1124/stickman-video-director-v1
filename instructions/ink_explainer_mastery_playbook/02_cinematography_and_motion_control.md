# 02: Cinematography & Motion Control

## 1. The 2.5s–4.0s Pacing Rule (The Retention Secret)
Short-form educational animations on YouTube and TikTok retain viewers because **a new visual concept or punchline lands every 2.5 to 4 seconds**.

### Rules for Shot Duration:
- **Hook Shot (Shot 1)**: 2.5s to 3.5s max. Must establish the paradox or question immediately.
- **Body Shots (Shots 2 to N-1)**: 3.0s to 4.0s per visual metaphor. Never exceed 5.0 seconds on a single visual.
- **Climax / Resolution Shot**: 3.5s to 4.5s with a memorable takeaway icon or posture.

## 2. Preventing AI Video Morphing & Hallucinations

AI video models love to turn 2D stickmen into photorealistic humans or 3D sculptures if motion is underspecified. To prevent morphing:

1. **Keep Actions Narrow and Specific**:
   - ❌ *"A stickman explores the complex nature of human relationships."* (AI will hallucinate 3D humans)
   - ✅ *"A 2D stickman shrugs his shoulders and points to a floating question mark on an off-white paper canvas."* (Clean, executable 2D action)
2. **Anchor the Background Constantly**:
   - Repeatedly state `"clean off-white paper canvas"` or `"minimalist cream background"`.
3. **Specify 2D Line Animation**:
   - Explicitly instruct `"hand-drawn 2D vector doodle animation, flat 2D lines only, zero 3D depth"`.
4. **Use Subtle Camera Moves**:
   - Fast pans or orbital 3D camera turns force the model into 3D rendering.
   - Use `"static eye-level camera"`, `"subtle push-in zoom"`, or `"snappy 1.1x punch-in"`.

## 3. Kinetic Visual Cues & Choreography

| Scene Type | Stickman Posture | Visual Metaphor | Sound FX Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook / Question** | Head tilted, one hand on chin, question mark | Giant ink `?` drawing above head | Subtle pen scribble + soft thud |
| **Emotional Contrast** | Stomping foot or crossed arms | Broken red ink heart vs gold pedestal | Dull impact thud + glass crack |
| **Brain / Logic** | Pointing finger upward | Rotating gear mechanism inside skull doodle | Watch ticking or mechanical click |
| **Social Pressure** | Stickman hunched under giant weight | Giant heavy black boulder labeled "Expectations" | Deep bass drop or rumble |
| **Mastery / Solution** | Standing tall, chest out, calm smile | Golden crown or glowing lightbulb icon | Clean chime bell or airy whoosh |
