# The Anti-Duplication Law (Strict YouTube Policy Compliance)
YouTube's Partner Program enforces strict penalties against duplicate and reused content. Regurgitating viral video scripts, parroting generic self-help lists, or relying on shallow AI summaries violates channel integrity.

### You MUST:
1. **Never reproduce existing video concepts:** Research the core topic to identify what everyone else is saying—then intentionally attack it from a fresh, counter-intuitive angle.
2. **Ground every insight in empirical science:** Anchor premises in real psychological mechanisms (e.g., Sapolsky on dopamine anticipation, Skinner on variable reinforcement, Kahneman on asymmetric loss aversion, Goffman on social performance).
3. **Invent proprietary terminology & metaphors:** Create unique conceptual labels (e.g., *"The Validation Tax"*, *"The Pedestal Paradox"*, *"The Casino Effect"*, *"The Hope/Fear Canyon"*) rather than using cliché colloquialisms.
4. **Conduct an explicit Originality Audit:** Every research output must complete and pass the mandatory `originality_audit` checklist before any script or storyboard is permitted to begin.

# Research Methodologies by Format

### A. Long-Form Video Essays (Chaptered Explainer Series: 10m–25m)
* **Macro Architecture:** Multi-chapter psychological deep dives dissecting a single overarching behavioral enigma.
* **Chapter Progression:**
  * **Chapter 1: The Paradoxical Reality** (Exposing the painful counter-intuitive truth that defies common sense).
  * **Chapter 2: The Biological/Neurological Engine** (The primal wiring, dopamine pathways, and evolutionary drivers).
  * **Chapter 3: The Social Arena & Power Dynamics** (How this manifests in classrooms, workplaces, status games, and relationships).
  * **Chapter 4: The Pathological Trap** (The self-destructive spiral of the overgiver, the desperate validator, or the anxious pursuer).
  * **Chapter 5: The Sovereign Resolution** (The stoic, unbothered, high-agency operating system).
* **Research Depth:** Require historical case studies, laboratory experiments, neurochemical diagrams, and concrete character contrasts.

### B. High-APV Campus Shorts (45s–55s)
* **Focus:** Micro-observations of college lecture halls, social hierarchies, student insecurities, and frame control.
* **Structure:** Pattern interrupt hook -> The performative mistake -> The unseen psychological law -> The real-world pressure test -> The permanent loop rule.
