# Studio Target Output Specification: The Real Ink Explainer Standard (Comic-Sync Edition)

> **MANDATE:** This specification defines the non-negotiable benchmark for all visual, audio, pacing, and consistency outputs produced by this studio. Reverse-engineered directly from broadcast forensics of *Ink Explainer* (`temp/Ink_Explainer_Sample_1.mp4`).

---

## 1. Executive Archetype & Strategy
* **Reference Channel:** *Ink Explainer* (and *Ink Explainer - Hindi*).
* **Niche:** College & Senior-Class Psychology, Campus Social Hierarchies, Dating Dynamics (The Silent Guy Effect, Backbencher Paradox, Female Teacher Perceptions).
* **Format:** 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920) YouTube Shorts / Reels.
* **Duration:** 45 to 75 seconds (dynamic, unbounded visual beats; 35 to 50+ story cuts as required by narrative quality).
* **Language:** English (United States) Narration with Kinetic SubStation Alpha Subtitles.
* **Cut & Frame Generation Mandate:** **100% Dynamic Story-Driven Cuts.** Zero fixed-interval cutting. Cut timing and frame generation count are determined purely by narrative shifts, character reactions, and rhetoric. Generate as many frames as required to tell the story with zero compromises on quality.

---

## 2. Visual Architecture (The Real Ink Explainer DNA)

### 2.1 The Nature of Animation (Static 2D Comic Panels)
* **99.5% Static Illustrations:** Zero character rigging, zero lip-sync, zero walk cycles, and zero AI video morphing.
* **The Illusion of Motion:** Created purely by dynamic, story-driven cuts (averaging ~1.5s - 1.8s, with punchy 0.7s micro-actions up to 2.4s dramatic pauses).

### 2.2 Color & Art Style (Minimal Hand-Drawn Comic Doodle Standard)
* **Unified 2D Comic Doodle Aesthetic:** Both character and environment share the EXACT same visual DNA: bold black ink pen lines (5px–8px stroke weight), flat color fills, and minimal hand-drawn doodle geometry.
* **Minimalist Hand-Drawn Environments:** Simple, charming 2D cartoon backgrounds matching the stick figure aesthetic:
  * *Lecture Hall / Classroom:* Simple cream wall, flat forest-green chalkboard with chalk doodle formulas, 2-3 cartoon wooden desks drawn with clean bold ink outlines. Zero 3D depth, zero complex anime perspective.
  * *Corridors & Lockers:* Flat floor, simple row of cartoon blue rectangular lockers with line vents, simple wooden door outline.
  * *Night / Outdoor:* Flat solid navy blue sky (`#1B263B`), 2-3 dot stars, simple cartoon fence, simple streetlamp with flat yellow light cone.
  * *Text / Concept Cards:* Clean off-white paper canvas (`#FAF9F6`) with bold hand-drawn ink lettering and hand-drawn arrows.
* **Strict Style Ban:** Zero hyper-detailed anime architecture, zero photorealistic wood reflections, zero 3D ceiling light arrays, zero gradient meshes. The background must look hand-drawn by the same cartoonist who drew the stickman.

### 2.3 The Character DNA (College Contextual Stickman)
* **Head:** Perfectly geometric circular white head (`#FFFFFF`) with uniform black outline.
* **Eyes & Eyebrows:** Simple capsule/dot eyes and expressive eyebrow angles that convey 100% of emotion (smirk, side-eye, curiosity, sleep, shock).
* **Body:** Classic black stick figure lines, dressed in modern campus wardrobe:
  * *The Silent Guy / Backbencher:* Dark hoodie / jacket, messy fringe hair, hands in pockets or resting chin on hand.
  * *The Frontbencher / Performer:* Glasses, eager raised hand.
  * *The Classmates / Girls:* Simple ponytail or bob hairstyles.
* **Hands:** White cartoon mitten-style hands with black contour for prop interactions (smartphones with screen glow, notebook pens, keys, door handles).

### 2.4 100% Character Consistency (Modular Asset Kit)
* Consistency is achieved by compositing pre-designed digital asset layers:
  `[Background Layer]` + `[Character Pose & Hair]` + `[Prop]` + `[Text Card]`
* **Zero style drift.** Characters maintain identical head sizes, line weights, and appearances across all 40 shots.

## 3. Pacing & Semantic Cut Timing (0.7s to 2.5s)

Visual cuts are strictly driven by **semantic spoken clauses**, never a fixed timer:
* **Micro-Action Verbs (0.7s – 1.0s):** Fast physical cuts (*"locks the door"*, *"slams book"*, *"flips switch"*).
* **Subject Introductions & Text Cards (1.2s – 1.6s):** Direct concepts (*"The Silent Trap"*, *"Rule #1"*).
* **Explanatory Clauses (1.8s – 2.5s):** Narrative psychological explanations.

---

## 4. Audio Mastering & Sound Design

* **Integrated Loudness:** Strictly normalized to **-11.9 LUFS** with a compressed dynamic range (**LRA: 2.0 LU**).
* **Voice Profile:** `en-US-ChristopherNeural` (Deep, calm, authoritative, articulate male narrator with American accent and crisp studio diction).
* **Background Music (BGM):** Continuous ambient lo-fi / synth drone bed at `-22dB` to `-24dB`.
* **Diegetic Foley:** Real-world sound effects synced to visual actions (footsteps, keys, locker slams, phone notification chimes, whispering).

---

## 5. Kinetic Subtitles

* **Format:** SubStation Alpha (`.ass`) burned in safe lower-third (`MarginV: 400`).
* **Styling:** ALL UPPERCASE `Montserrat ExtraBold` or `Arial Black`.
* **Active Spoken Word:** Vivid Gold pop (`&H0000D7FF`) at 108% scale.
* **Inactive Words:** Solid White (`&H00FFFFFF`) with 4.5px black outline.
* **Language:** English only.
