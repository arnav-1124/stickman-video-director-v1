# Long-Form Animated Short Films Rule (Day One Films Signature Architecture)

## 1. Overview & Positioning
For all long-form episodic chapters (2 to 5 minutes) on **Sticky in Dark**, the packaging and presentation must adhere to the high-CTR, high-retention standard inspired by top-performing animated channels like **Day One Films**. 

Never use academic jargon, textbook labels, or generic video-essay phrasing. Every film must feel raw, human, relatable, and cinematic.

---

## 2. YouTube Title Standards

### 2.1 The Signature Formula
Every long-form episode title MUST follow this exact structure:
```text
[PUNCHY STATEMENT / CORE PAIN POINT / PROVOCATION] | An Animated Short Film (Chapter X)
```

### 2.2 Title Rules
1. **The Lead Hook:**
   - Must point directly at an everyday, visceral human experience, mistake, or anxiety that millions of people experience in silence.
   - Examples of approved lead hooks:
     - ✅ `YOU REPLIED INSTANTLY - Problem?`
     - ✅ `YOU REPLIED INSTANTLY`
     - ✅ `LEFT ON READ`
     - ✅ `CHASING`
     - ✅ `THE CHASE`
     - ✅ `DISCIPLINE`
     - ✅ `DOPAMINE`
2. **Episodic Indicator:**
   - Include `(Chapter X)` or `(Part X)` at the end to create narrative momentum, signaling to the viewer that this is part of an overarching serialized story.
3. **Banned in Long-Form Titles:**
   - ❌ Academic jargon (e.g. `The Pedestal Paradox`, `Variable Intermittent Reinforcement`, `Cognitive Dissonance`).
   - ❌ Clickbait spam labels (e.g. `Alpha vs Sigma`, `Dark Psychology 101`, `High Value Man`).
   - ❌ Punctuation salad (e.g. `MUST WATCH!!!`, multiple emojis).

## 3. Thumbnail Architecture & Visual Standards

### 3.1 Layout & Visual Canvas
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Widescreen (`1920×1080` Full HD).
* **Atmospheric Mood:** High contrast, moody, minimalist 2D ink style on off-white or dark background.
* **The Emotional Scene:** A single, relatable physical scenario highlighting isolation or emotional tension:
  - *Example 1:* The 2:00 AM dark bedroom, blankets pulled up, phone screen glowing onto the tired stickman's face with the red clock glowing on the nightstand.
  - *Example 2:* The solitary desk under a single warm task lamp.
  - *Example 3:* Walking away down an empty street into the sunset while others watch.

### 3.2 Thumbnail Text Rules
* **Text Selection:** 2 to 4 words maximum. Direct mirror of the title hook (e.g., `YOU REPLIED INSTANTLY - Problem?`, `LEFT ON READ.`, `STOP CHASING.`).
* **Typography:** Bold, textured, hand-drawn vector ink lettering (e.g., `Permanent Marker`, `Bangers`, or custom hand-inked typography).
* **Composition & Margins:**
  - **Centered & Balanced:** The text must be centered in its dedicated negative space zone.
  - **Generous Breathing Room:** At least 80px–100px padding from canvas edges and character figures.
  - **Zero Crowding:** Never place text over character faces or critical background props.

---

## 4. Narrative Pacing & Subtitle Standards

### 4.1 Natural Cadence Policy
* Default to **100% natural, unhurried, conversational pacing** from the initial generation.
* Pauses between dialogue thoughts must feel organic, reflective, and confident. Never rush sentences or clip the narrator's vocal decay.

### 4.2 Outro Teaser & Forward Hook Law
* Every chapter must close with an **Open Loop / Forward Hook** teasing the next chapter (e.g., Slide 37 introducing *Chapter 02: "The Casino Effect"*).
* **Elevated Outro Subtitle Rule (`OutroWordSub`):**
  - On slides containing bottom-anchored graphic elements (such as `[ SUBSCRIBE FOR PART 2 ]` buttons), subtitles MUST use elevated positioning (`MarginV=240`, font size `46pt`) rather than standard lower-third (`MarginV=110`).
  - This guarantees that animated subtitles float cleanly in the negative space without colliding with or obscuring the call-to-action button.
* **Visual Hold & Fadeout:**
  - Provide a **1.5s to 2.0s silent visual hold** after narration completes before the cut, allowing the viewer to absorb the call to action while the background music fades out smoothly (`afade=t=out:d=3.0`).

## 5. Metadata Deliverable Checklist (`youtube_publishing_kit.md`)
Every long-form release must compile a complete publishing kit containing:
1. **Primary Title:** Following the Day One Films signature formula.
2. **Alternative Titles:** 3–4 minimalist and pain-point variants.
3. **Master Video Deliverable:** Clickable link to CFR 25fps rendered master with LUFS and file size metrics.
4. **Thumbnail File:** Clickable link to verified 1920×1080 master graphic.
5. **Description Block:**
   - 3-paragraph emotional hook setup.
   - Complete timestamps for all chapters/scenes.
   - "Question for the Comments" engagement trigger.
   - Next chapter preview & subscription CTA.
6. **Tags:** 15–20 focused psychology, storytelling, and animation tags.
7. **Pinned Comment:** Strategic comment prompting debate and driving series subscriptions.
