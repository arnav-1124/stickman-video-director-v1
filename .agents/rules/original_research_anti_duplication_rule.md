# Original Deep Research & YouTube Anti-Duplication Rule (Mandatory Standard)

## 1. The YouTube Policy Mandate (Zero-Tolerance for Reused/Duplicate Content)
YouTube's Partner Program (YPP) strictly penalizes and demonetizes channels that produce:
* **Duplicate Content:** Re-packaging popular scripts, parroting viral TikToks/Reels, or regurgitating ubiquitous "Top 5" self-help tips.
* **Reused Content:** Content that does not provide significant original commentary, unique educational framing, or substantial new value.
* **Superficial AI Syntheses:** Shallow text scrapes or summaries that say what hundreds of other creators have already said.

**STRICT LAW: No video, chapter, script, or storyboard may ever be initiated without completing Stage 1: Original Deep Research with a verified Originality Audit. Skipping this step is strictly prohibited under all circumstances.**

---

## 2. The 5 Pillars of Original Research

Every production must be the product of **authentic, deep-dive research** fulfilling all five criteria:

### I. The Counter-Intuitive Thesis (Pattern Interrupt)
* Do NOT validate obvious platitudes (e.g., *"confidence is key"*, *"work hard"*, *"play hard to get"*).
* The core premise must challenge conventional wisdom or present an overlooked psychological paradox (e.g., *The Pedestal Paradox: Elevating someone forces them to look down on you*; *The Casino Effect: Dopamine spikes on uncertainty, not the prize*).

### II. Empirical & Scientific Grounding
* Ideas must be anchored in verified behavioral science, neurobiology, evolutionary psychology, or classic sociological experiments:
  * e.g., B.F. Skinner (Operant Conditioning & Variable-Ratio Schedules)
  * Robert Sapolsky (Dopamine Anticipation vs. Reward Reception)
  * Erving Goffman (Dramaturgical Self & Social Posturing)
  * Amos Tversky & Daniel Kahneman (Loss Aversion & Asymmetric Valuation)
* Abstract claims must always be tied to the exact biological or neurological mechanism driving the behavior.

### III. Proprietary Intellectual Metaphors & Visual Models
* Avoid generic visual tropes. Create unique cognitive anchors and proprietary terms:
  * Instead of "dating is unpredictable," use **"The Variable Lever"** or **"The Unresolved Gap"**.
  * Instead of "being too nice," use **"The Validation Tax"** or **"The Overgiver's Chasm"**.
  * Use original visual diagrams (e.g., dopamine comparison curves, rubber stamp triads, lever tally montages).

### IV. Real-World Sociological Depth (Micro-Observations)
* Ground abstract psychology in hyper-specific, relatable real-world vignettes:
  * The student who stays up until 3:00 AM sweating slide fonts vs. the student who speaks in three calm sentences.
  * The frantic phone refresher checking notification timestamps.
  * The micro-mechanics of classroom and relationship frame control.

### V. Actionable Sovereignty (The Non-Preachy Takeaway)
* Videos must never end with generic motivational slogans.
* The climax must deliver a pragmatic, stoic rule of personal sovereignty that reframes the viewer's mental model permanently.

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Scripting Originality Audit Gate

Before any `storyboard.json`, script, or visual prompt file is authored, the researcher must produce and verify `creative_research.json` containing the following explicit audit:

```json
"originality_audit": {
  "is_duplicate_or_paraphrased": false,
  "unique_intellectual_angle": "Detailed statement of why this exact perspective does NOT exist on YouTube/TikTok.",
  "banned_cliches_purged": [
    "List of common cliches explicitly excluded from the narrative"
  ],
  "original_metaphors_introduced": [
    "Proprietary metaphors, terminology, and visual mechanisms designed specifically for this episode"
  ],
  "academic_or_empirical_sources": [
    "Scientific principles, behavioral studies, or psychological foundations used"
  ]
}
```

---

## 4. Enforcement & Pipeline Violation Rules
1. **Gatekeeper Check:** The `storyboard-director` and `prompt-engineer` MUST refuse to create beats or prompts if `creative_research.json` lacks an `originality_audit` or if the audit flags any duplicate/unoriginal elements.
2. **Plagiarism Zero-Tolerance:** Lifting narrative structures, verbatim phrasing, or distinctive visual setups from existing creators immediately voids the project.
3. **Applies to All Formats:** This rule governs both short-form campus psychological shorts (50s) and long-form chaptered psychological explainers (10m–20m).
