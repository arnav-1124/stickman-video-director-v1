# 60-Second Retention Script Rule

## 1. The 5-Beat Retention Clock
Every script must fit the strict 60-second retention structure:
- **Beat 1 (0s–3s) - Pattern Interrupt Hook**: Counter-intuitive opening that destroys a common assumption. (Word count: ~8-12 words).
- **Beat 2 (3s–18s) - The Cognitive Mechanism**: Scientific or psychological root cause (Spotlight Effect, Amygdala Hijack, Sunk Cost). (Word count: ~35-40 words).
- **Beat 3 (18s–42s) - The Stickman Contrast**: Visual comparison of Person A (reactive/weak) vs Person B (calm/strategic). (Word count: ~50-55 words).
- **Beat 4 (42s–55s) - The Actionable Rule**: The single takeaway rule the viewer can implement today. (Word count: ~25-30 words).
- **Beat 5 (55s–60s) - The Seamless Loop**: Grammatically flows back into the opening hook sentence. (Word count: ~10 words).

## 2. Cadence & Constraints
- Total script word count: **135 to 148 words** (~2.3 words/sec).
- Tone: Mature, articulate, calm, psychologically penetrating. No cartoonish tropes or childish banter.
