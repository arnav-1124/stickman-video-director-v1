# Ink Noir Visual Style & Alan Becker Physics Rule

## 1. Canvas & Palette
- **Background**: Deep Charcoal / Dark Slate (`#0B0B0E`) with subtle paper texture grain. Never use pure digital `#000000` or flat plain white.
- **Character**: Pure high-contrast white vector line art (`#FFFFFF`), uniform 6px-8px stroke width.
- **Selective Color Shock (The 1-Color Rule)**:
  - **Crimson Red (`#FF2A4D`)**: Danger, ego, perceived social anxiety, manipulation traps.
  - **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: Truth, emotional mastery, cognitive clarity, detachment.
  - Never mix 3+ accent colors. Retain 90% monochrome discipline.

## 2. Alan Becker 2D Character Physics
- **Anticipation**: Before any fast movement, character crouches or leans in the opposite direction.
- **Squash and Stretch**: When landing, stomping, or impacted, character compresses vertically and widens horizontally.
- **Physical Weight**: Feet plant firmly on an invisible ground baseline (Y: ~1400px). No floaty gliding.
- **Expressive Silhouette**: Head tilt, shoulder hunch (defeat/shame), chest expansion (power/control), arm cross (emotional detachment).

## 3. Cinematography & Lighting
- **Spotlight Cones**: Dramatic radial gradient spotlights centered on the stickman during isolation or revelation beats.
- **Camera Moves**: Snappy 1.2x punch-in zooms on key psychological revelations, followed by gentle easing.
