# Ink Explainer Visual Style Rule (Minimal Hand-Drawn 2D Comic Standard)

> **CORE PHILOSOPHY: 100% STYLISTIC HARMONY BETWEEN CHARACTER AND ENVIRONMENT**
> The character is a minimalist 2D stickman figure drawn with simple, clean black ink pen strokes. Therefore, the environment **MUST** be drawn in the **exact same minimal hand-drawn cartoon / doodle style**. 
> Never use hyper-detailed anime backgrounds, 3D architectural renders, complex vanishing-point grids, or realistic lighting reflections. The background must look like it came from the exact same pen and sketchbook as the stickman!

---

## 1. Environment Aesthetic: Minimal Hand-Drawn Cartoon Doodle
1. **Minimalist 2D Vector / Doodle Line Art:**
   * Drawn with bold black ink pen lines (4px–7px stroke weight) with slight organic hand-drawn warmth.
   * Minimalist geometric forms: simple cartoon rectangles for desks, clean flat rectangles for chalkboards and doors, simple cartoon window frames.
2. **Flat, Simple Color Fills (Zero Photorealism):**
   * Solid, clean pastel or earthy flat color blocks (e.g. warm tan desks, soft cream walls `#FAF9F6`, flat forest-green chalkboard, soft sky blue).
   * **Zero complex shading, zero gradient meshes, zero realistic wood grain reflections, zero ceiling light glare.**
3. **Restrained Visual Elements (Less is More):**
   * *Classroom / Lecture Hall:* Just 2 or 3 simple cartoon wooden desks, a flat green chalkboard on a cream wall with a couple of doodle chalk formulas, and an optional simple cartoon window.
   * *Corridor:* A flat wall, a simple row of 3 or 4 flat blue rectangular lockers with cartoon vent lines, a simple wooden door.
   * *Night / Dorm / Street:* A flat solid navy sky with a few dot stars, a simple cartoon wooden fence, a simple lamp pole with a flat yellow light triangle.
4. **Negative Constraints (Strictly Banned):**
   * BANNED: Hyper-detailed architectural perspective, photorealistic textures, dozens of tiered auditorium rows, realistic lighting fixtures/tubes, anime visual-novel backgrounds.

---

## 2. Character Anatomy & Consistency
1. **Head:** Smooth circular white shape (`#FFFFFF`) with uniform 6.5px black vector ink outline.
2. **Eyes & Eyebrows:** 
   * Simple dark capsules/dots or curved cartoon arcs.
   * Eyebrow angles convey 100% of the emotion (unbothered, calm, curious, stressed).
   * Simple horizontal line for mouth (deadpan, subtle half-smile, or open cartoon gasp).
3. **Body & Wardrobe:**
   * Simple bold black stick figure limbs.
   * Minimalist cartoon clothing: oversized flat-color hoodie (dark charcoal), glasses/collared shirt for frontbencher, simple beige sweater/ponytail for girl.
   * Cartoon white mitten hands with black outline.
4. **Purity of Canvas:**
   * Master assets and scene panels must contain **zero unwanted text, zero labels, zero color swatches, zero UI elements**.

---

## 3. Visual Composition Law
* **Characters and Environments Must Never Clash.** If you take a stick figure and drop it into a scene, it must look like a natural, intentional, unified 2D webcomic panel. 
* High contrast, bold outlines, flat color blocking, off-white textured paper feel (`#FAF9F6`).
