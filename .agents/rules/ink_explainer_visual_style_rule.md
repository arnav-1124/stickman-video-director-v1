# Ink Explainer Visual Style Rule (The Paint Explainer Aesthetic)

## 1. Canvas & Atmosphere
- **Canvas Texture**: Crisp, warm off-white textured paper (`#FAF9F6` or `#F4F3EE`). Never pure `#000000` or plain `#FFFFFF`.
- **Contrast**: High contrast black ink pen strokes (`#0A0D14`) on the warm paper surface.

## 2. Character Anatomy & Rules
- **Head**: Perfectly smooth circular white shape with a clean black ink contour.
- **Limbs & Torso**: Clean 2D line strokes (6px-8px stroke weight), fluid joints.
- **Faces**: Simple, ultra-expressive doodle eyes (dots, slight curves, eyebrow angles). Never realistic human eyes or mouth openings.
- **Physical Weight & Posing**: Feet touch an invisible ground line; poses communicate psychological state (shrugging, hands on chin, crossed arms, pointing).

## 3. Visual Metaphors & Props
- Hand-drawn ink line art doodle props (balance scales, lightbulbs, brains with gears, chains, crowns, pedestals, question marks).
- Every visual metaphor directly clarifies the psychological voiceover.

## 4. The 1-Color Accent Rule
- 95% of every scene is monochrome (black ink on off-white paper).
- At most **ONE** accent color per scene:
  - **Crimson Red (`#FF2A4D`)**: Danger, rejection, ego, toxic behavior, anxiety.
  - **Electric Cyan / Gold (`#00F0FF` / `#FFD700`)**: Clarity, truth, high status, emotional detachment.
- Never use full multi-color gradients or 3+ colors in a single shot.
