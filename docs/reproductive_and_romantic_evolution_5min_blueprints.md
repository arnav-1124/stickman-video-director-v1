### 2. The Core Scientific & Anthropological Truth
* **The 270-Day Disconnect:** In primal nature, cause and effect happen immediately (touch fire $\rightarrow$ burn). A 9-month (270-day) gap between intimacy and childbirth made causal connection impossible for 100,000+ years.
* **Spirit Babies (Virgin Birth Anthropology):** Isolated tribes (Trobriand Islanders, Australian Aborigines) genuinely believed babies were placed in mothers by water spirits (*baloma*) when swimming or eating sacred fruit.
* **Partible Paternity:** Ancient Amazonian tribes (Aché, Yanomami) believed a fetus was built from the accumulated contributions of *multiple* men—giving a child 3–4 "fathers" who all provided meat.
* **The Goat Pen Epiphany (~10,000 BCE):** The Agricultural Revolution cracked the secret. Fencing male rams away from female sheep stopped all reproduction. Bringing the ram back produced lambs 5 months later, sparking the realization that humans operate the same way.

* **The 3 Primal Radars of Attraction:**
  1. *The Scent Test (MHC Genes):* Natural body odor communicates immune system genetics (Major Histocompatibility Complex); humans are drawn to mates with complementary immune defenses.
  2. *Symmetry & Health:* Clear skin, symmetrical features, and shiny hair as un-fakeable parasite shields.
  3. *The First Courtship Gifts:* High-status hunters bringing prized honeycombs or rare seashells to prove provisioning loyalty.
* **Love as Temporary Madness:** Brain scans prove falling in love floods the VTA with dopamine, crashes serotonin by 40% (mirroring clinical OCD obsession), and shuts off the amygdala (blinding the brain to red flags).
