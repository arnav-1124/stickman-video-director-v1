## 2. Channel Keywords (Settings → Channel → Basic info)

> **Location in YouTube Studio:**  
> **YouTube Studio** → **Settings** (Bottom Left Gear Icon) → **Channel** → **Basic Info** tab → **Keywords** box.  
> *(Paste as comma-separated values. YouTube will automatically turn them into tags).*

### 📋 Copy & Paste Text:

```text
ancient humans, prehistoric humans, human evolution, anthropology, ancient history, archaeology, stone age, human behavior, psychology, why humans lie, how humans survived, ancient daily life, evolutionary biology, animated explainer, stickman animation, history documentary, ink explainer, prehistoric survival, paleolithic, sticky in dark
```

### Why these exact keywords work:
1. **`ancient humans` + `prehistoric humans`**: Directly indexes your channel into YouTube's top recommended video carousel alongside `@Inkexplainer96`.
2. **`human evolution` + `anthropology`**: Connects your content to educational and documentary authority authority graphs.
3. **`animated explainer` + `ink explainer`**: Tells YouTube's suggestion engine what format you produce so you appear in "Up Next" queues.

## 3. Upload Defaults (Settings → Upload defaults)

> **Location in YouTube Studio:**  
> **Settings** → **Upload Defaults** → Configure once so every upload starts pre-configured.

### A. Basic Info:
- **Visibility:** `Unlisted`  
  *(Crucial Rule: NEVER upload directly as Public. Always upload as Unlisted to allow YouTube to finish 1080p VP09/AV1 processing, run automated copyright checks, and calibrate captions before publishing).*

### B. Advanced Settings tab:
- **Category:** `Education` *(Matches @Inkexplainer96, Kurzgesagt, and TED-Ed).*
- **License:** `Standard YouTube License`
- **Video Language:** `English`
- **Caption Certification:** `None`
- **Title and Description Language:** `English`
- **Comments:** `On` → `Pause comments` = OFF, `Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review` = ON.
- **Show how many viewers like this video:** `Checked (Enabled)`

## 4. Channel Customization (Branding & Layout)

> **Location in YouTube Studio:**  
> **YouTube Studio** → **Customization** (Left Sidebar) → **Branding** tab.

### 1. Profile Picture:
- **Dimensions:** 800 × 800 px (displays at 98 × 98 px).
- **Design:** Clean white vector stickman head with dark charcoal ink lines (`#0A0D14`) on a high-contrast dark indigo or golden-savanna backdrop. Must be instantly recognizable at tiny 32px comment sizes.

### 2. Banner Image:
- **Dimensions:** 2560 × 1440 px (Safe zone for mobile: 1235 × 338 px in center).
- **Text on Banner:**  
  `STICKY IN DARK`  
  *The Hidden History of Human Nature & Survival*  
  `NEW EPISODES WEEKLY`

### 3. Video Watermark:
- **Dimensions:** 150 × 150 px (PNG with transparent background).
- **Display Time:** `Entire video` (Bottom-right corner).
- **Visual:** A small circular subscribe badge or channel logo.

## 5. Algorithmic Compounding Strategy

1. **Category Consistency:** Always maintain `Education`. Deviating to *Entertainment* or *People & Blogs* confuses YouTube's topic clusters.
2. **First 48-Hour Retention Loop:** When uploading a new long-form film:
   - Pin a provocative question in the comments.
   - Reply to the first 20 comments within 2 hours of publishing.
   - Link your top-performing Shorts directly to this video using YouTube Studio's **"Related Video"** selector to siphon vertical traffic into horizontal watch hours.
